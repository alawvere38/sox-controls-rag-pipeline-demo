# Information Security Policy

**Company:** Crestline Manufacturing, Inc.
**Document ID:** POL-SEC-001
**Version:** 5.0
**Policy owner:** Chief Information Security Officer
**Approved by:** Audit Committee
**Last reviewed:** January 2026

## Purpose
This policy establishes the minimum security requirements for protecting Crestline's information
systems and data, including systems relevant to financial reporting.

## Scope
Applies to all employees, contractors, and third parties accessing Crestline systems, and to all
company-owned and managed infrastructure.

## Policy statements

**1. Authentication.** All users must authenticate with a unique individual account. Shared
accounts are prohibited except for approved service accounts. Multi-factor authentication is
required for all remote access and for access to financially significant systems.

**2. Passwords.** Passwords must be a minimum of 14 characters. Service account credentials are
rotated at least every 90 days. Credentials must never be shared or stored in plaintext.

**3. Least privilege.** Access is granted on a least-privilege basis. Users receive only the access
required to perform their role. Privileged access is restricted, logged, and reviewed.

**4. Acceptable use.** Company systems are for authorized business purposes. Users must not disable
security controls or install unauthorized software.

**5. Data classification.** Data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential, or Restricted.
Financial reporting data is classified at minimum as Confidential and must not be transmitted to
unapproved third-party services.

**6. Logging and monitoring.** Security-relevant events on in-scope systems are logged and retained
for at least 12 months and reviewed for indicators of compromise.

**7. Incident response.** Suspected security incidents must be reported to IT Security immediately
and are handled under the Incident Response Plan.

## Exceptions
Exceptions require documented risk acceptance approved by the CISO and are reviewed at least
annually.

## Enforcement
Violations may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.
